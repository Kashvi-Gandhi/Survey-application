🛡️ QuizPulse: Admin Dashboard & System Management Specification
1. Executive Summary & Core Objectives
The Admin Dashboard grants system administrators complete oversight over all platform users (surveyors), master template assets (question banks), and active surveys across the application without altering core user role definitions.

2. High-Level Admin Capabilities
👥 A. Surveyor (User) Management
Surveyor Directory Table:
Displays a full list of all registered surveyors on the platform.
Columns: Surveyor Name, Email, Joined Date, Total Surveys Created, Total Responses Received, Account Status (is_active), and Actions.

Account Status Toggle (is_active):
Admins can activate or deactivate any surveyor account via an interactive status switch.
Behavior: Setting is_active = false immediately revokes the surveyor's session and prevents them from logging in or modifying surveys.

Account Deletion / Cleanup:
Option to permanently remove inactive or spam surveyor accounts.

📚 B. Master Question Bank Management
Admin-Managed Templates:
Admins can Create, Update, and Delete official, platform-wide Question Banks.
Categorize banks (e.g., Human Resources, Academic Feedback, Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)).

Question Bank Inventory:
Add, edit, or delete individual questions within any Master Question Bank.
Surveyor Access: Surveyors can view and clone questions from these official banks directly into their own custom surveys.

📋 C. Global Survey Moderation & Management
System-Wide Survey Registry:
A master view showing every survey created on the platform, regardless of who created it.
Displays Survey Title, Creator Name/Email, Creation Date, Response Count, and Public Link Status.

Conditional Survey Editing:
Admins have edit permissions over all surveys, subject to the safety rule: Surveys can only be edited if response_count == 0.
Survey Deletion & Closure:
Admins can delete any survey that violates platform guidelines or is marked as spam.
Admins can force-close/archive an active survey to stop receiving new submissions ahead of schedule.

📊 D. Platform Metrics & Analytics Cards
The top header of the Admin Dashboard will feature summary overview cards:
Total Surveyors: Count of active vs. inactive surveyor accounts.
Total Surveys: Total number of surveys created across the system.
Total Submissions: Cumulative response count recorded across all surveys.
Master Question Banks: Total number of global template banks available.
